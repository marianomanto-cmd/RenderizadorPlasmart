The mono, uppercase, wide-tracked label that introduces a section — Plasmart's status-line kicker, with a glowing indigo dot.

```jsx
<Kicker>Córdoba · AR — Desde 2006</Kicker>
<Kicker dot={false}>Nº/ 03 — Signal</Kicker>
```

Keep the text short and uppercase; it reads like a readout, not a sentence.
